As a SURE undergraduate researcher at the Aerospace Mechatronics Lab in summer 2023, I worked on two pieces of the lab's push toward autonomous timber-harvesting machines.

**Grapple characterization from images.** I created the datasets used to train a multi-stage CNN for grapple and log segmentation, grapple-opening prediction and log counting, and integrated a draw-wire sensor on the grapple to validate the network's opening estimates experimentally.

**Anti-sway testbed.** I designed the experimental setup that emulates a log-loader manipulator and its suspended grapple, which was used to validate an anti-sway trajectory planner. That work was published at ICRA 2025 {% cite jebellat2025antisway %}.
